Website policies

Effective October 1, 2026

The Pink Room is a free personal photobooth website maintained by Vivien Alba. These policies describe the current booth and the choices you have when using it.

PrivacyCookiesTermsRefunds

Privacy Policy

Camera, microphone and your media

Your browser asks for camera permission when you enable the camera. Microphone access is optional and requested only when you choose to include sound in a video. Camera images and microphone audio are processed in your browser to create your strip or movie. The booth does not upload these photos, recordings or audio to the website owner, a server or an analytics service.

Storage and deletion

Captured media stays in the current page session until you replace it, reload the page or close it. The booth has no account system, online gallery or server backup. Downloads are saved to your device and remain there until you delete them. Your browser, operating system or personal backup settings may separately retain files. The website owner cannot recover or delete your downloaded media.

Your choices

You can decline camera or microphone permission, switch sound off before recording, use the Turn camera off control, or close the page. You can revoke permissions in your browser settings. To clear the booth session, reload or close the page. Only photograph or record people who agree to participate, and obtain their permission before sharing the result.

Hosting and external websites

Although the booth does not send your media away, the hosting provider receives website requests and may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details for delivery and security. When this website is accessed through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The private ChatGPT hosted preview is subject to OpenAI’s Privacy Policy. External links follow the destination website’s policies.

Questions and privacy concerns

The site owner is Vivien Alba. You can report a website problem through the project’s GitHub issues. Issues are public, so do not post private photos, recordings or other sensitive information there. Questions about hosting records should be directed to the relevant hosting provider using its privacy contact.

Cookie Policy

The photobooth code does not set cookies or use local storage to save your media or preferences. It contains no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, embedded social feeds or externally loaded fonts. Fonts and decorative images are served with the website.

The hosting platform may use its own service or security technologies, particularly in the private signed in preview. These are governed by the hosting provider’s policies. Browser camera and microphone permissions are separate from cookies and remain under your browser’s control.

If the booth later adds optional tracking or other features that use cookies, this policy will be updated and appropriate choices provided before those features run.

Terms and Conditions

Use this booth lawfully and respectfully. Do not record people without permission, invade their privacy, harass them or use their images deceptively. A parent or guardian should supervise children using the camera or microphone.

You retain any rights you already hold in your photos and recordings. Using the booth does not transfer ownership of your media to the website owner. You are responsible for having permission to capture and share the people and content in your media.

The booth is provided free for personal use. Availability, camera access, downloads and video formats depend on your device and browser. Save any media you want to keep before closing the page. The website owner cannot restore unsaved files and does not promise uninterrupted operation or compatibility with every device.

Fonts are provided under their included open font licenses. Decorative assets and website materials do not give you rights to another person’s image or third party content. Nothing in these terms removes rights or protections that apply under law.

These policies may be updated when the website changes. The effective date identifies the current version.

Refund Policy

This website does not sell products, charge for downloads, accept payments or offer subscriptions. There are no website purchases to cancel or refund. Printing or other services you arrange elsewhere follow that provider’s terms. If paid features are introduced, their prices and refund terms will be explained before payment.
